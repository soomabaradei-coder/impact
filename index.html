About

Artificial intelligence is my craft: deep learning, explainable AI, and intelligent agents. What I care about is what it changes. A model matters to me when a disease is caught earlier, a pilgrim's journey gets easier, a student wins an opportunity, or a company learns to stand on its own.

That is the thread through my work. In health, my models read the body's signals so disease can be found early, and explain their reasoning to the clinicians who act on them. For Hajj and Umrah, which is personal to me as a daughter of Mecca, I directed the Training and Awareness Track of the Nusuk Card initiative and filed a patent for an AI kiosk serving pilgrims. And across education and business, I lead KITE to carry King Abdulaziz University's students toward global opportunities, chair the Rhodes Scholarship Preparation Initiative, grow young talent with Mawhiba, and advise organizations and individuals building real AI capability.

People outlast papers. The impact I care about keeps walking after I stop.
